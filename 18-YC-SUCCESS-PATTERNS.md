# 18 — YC Success Patterns
YC's major successes span marketplaces, fintech, developer infrastructure, consumer products and AI. Examples include Airbnb, Stripe, Coinbase, DoorDash, Dropbox and GitLab.

### Recurring mechanisms
1. Large potential market.
2. Strong founder/team.
3. Rapid product improvement.
4. Distribution or network effects.
5. Technical/operational leverage.
6. A narrow wedge that can expand.

### Selection-bias warning
Studying unicorns tells us what successful YC companies look like. It does **not** tell us what caused YC to select them.

### Correct research design
Compare winners with same-batch, same-sector companies that failed or remained small.

**Source:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies
