# Venture Atlas
# 09 - Market Opportunity Framework

Version 0.1 (Research Scaffold)

---

# Purpose

This framework defines how Venture Atlas evaluates the attractiveness of a startup market. Great companies are often built in great markets, but timing, structure, and customer demand matter as much as size.

---

# Key Questions

- Is the market large enough to support a venture-scale company?
- Is the market growing?
- What macro trends drive demand?
- Why is this the right time to enter?

---

# Market Evaluation

## Total Addressable Market (TAM)
Estimate the full long-term revenue opportunity.

## Serviceable Available Market (SAM)
Identify the realistic segment the startup can serve.

## Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
Estimate the share that could reasonably be captured.

---

# Market Characteristics

Evaluate:

- Growth rate
- Fragmentation
- Customer urgency
- Switching costs
- Regulatory environment
- Technology adoption
- AI disruption potential

---

# Competitive Landscape

Document:

- Established incumbents
- High-growth startups
- Open-source alternatives
- Substitute solutions

---

# Timing Analysis

Assess whether current trends create a favorable window.

Examples:

- AI breakthroughs
- Regulatory changes
- Cost reductions
- Consumer behavior shifts
- Infrastructure improvements

---

# White Space

Identify areas where:

- Customer needs remain underserved
- Existing products are inadequate
- Technology enables new business models

---

# Market Scorecard

Rate 1–10 with justification:

- Market Size
- Growth
- Timing
- Competition
- Customer Pain
- AI Opportunity
- Long-Term Potential

---

# Deliverable

Every startup profile should include a structured market analysis using this framework.
