# Venture Atlas
# 08 - Founder Evaluation Framework

Version 0.1 (Research Scaffold)

---

# Purpose

Exceptional startups are usually built by exceptional founders. This framework defines how founder quality will be evaluated consistently across every company in the Venture Atlas.

---

# Core Question

Why did investors believe **this team** was uniquely positioned to solve **this problem**?

---

# Evaluation Dimensions

## Founder-Market Fit
- Personal experience with the problem
- Domain expertise
- Credibility within the industry

## Technical Capability
- Engineering depth
- Research background
- Ability to build quickly

## Execution Ability
- Speed of shipping
- Learning velocity
- Resourcefulness

## Ambition
- Size of vision
- Willingness to tackle difficult problems
- Long-term commitment

## Leadership
- Hiring ability
- Team building
- Communication

---

# Supporting Evidence

Collect when available:
- Founder interviews
- Previous startups
- Open-source contributions
- Academic research
- Industry experience
- Publications
- Public talks

---

# Warning Signs

- Weak founder-market fit
- No technical differentiation
- Pure trend chasing
- Lack of execution history
- Unclear ownership of the problem

---

# Founder Scorecard

Rate 1–10 with justification:

- Founder-Market Fit
- Technical Excellence
- Execution Speed
- Product Intuition
- Leadership
- Vision
- Adaptability
- Overall Founder Strength

---

# Deliverable

Every startup profile will include a concise founder assessment using this framework, clearly separating observed facts from analytical inference.

---

# Status

Framework approved for all future founder evaluations.
