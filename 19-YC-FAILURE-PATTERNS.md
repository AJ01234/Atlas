# 19 — YC Failure Patterns
Public shutdown cases point to recurring mechanisms:
- premature scaling
- weak product-market fit
- regulatory exposure
- capital intensity
- hardware execution risk
- weak defensibility
- difficult unit economics

These are **case-derived patterns**, not proven portfolio-wide causal effects.

### Founder Pitfall Factor™
| Risk | Question |
|---|---|
| PMF | Are users repeatedly demanding it? |
| Capital | Must large capital be spent before validation? |
| Regulation | Can regulation kill or delay the model? |
| Scaling | Is hiring ahead of evidence? |
| Defensibility | Can incumbents copy it? |
| Concentration | Is one customer/channel carrying the business? |

### Falsifiability
Run this rubric retrospectively on successful and failed YC companies and test whether it separates them better than chance.

**Source:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies
