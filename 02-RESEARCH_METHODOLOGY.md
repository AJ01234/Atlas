# Why this document exists

The Venture Atlas is not intended to be another startup directory.

Thousands of websites already list startups, founders, funding rounds, and valuations.

Our objective is different.

We want to answer one question:

> "Why did elite investors decide this company deserved venture capital?"

To answer that question consistently, every chapter in this Atlas follows the same research framework.

---

# Research Philosophy

## 1. Evidence over opinion
Every statement should be supported by evidence whenever possible.

## 2. Separate facts from analysis
Facts answer "What happened?"
Analysis answers "Why did it happen?"

## 3. Consistency
Every startup is evaluated using exactly the same framework.

## 4. First Principles
Study the underlying problem before the solution.

## 5. Long-Term Thinking
Evaluate long-term potential rather than short-term popularity.

---

# Evidence Hierarchy

## Official
- Company website
- Investor announcement
- YC profile
- Founder interview

## Verified
- Reputable news
- Industry databases
- Research reports

## Inference
Clearly labeled original analysis.

---

# Standard Research Workflow

1. Identify startup
2. Gather official sources
3. Gather verified sources
4. Understand customer
5. Analyze market
6. Identify competitors
7. Infer investment thesis
8. Score startup
