# 15 — YC Application Playbook
YC's application guidance makes **clarity** unusually explicit.

### What YC says
- Be exceptionally clear and concise.
- Answer the product question directly.
- Avoid marketing language.
- Show what each founder has actually built or achieved.
- Demonstrate insight into the problem.
- Be specific rather than generic.

### Important empirical correction
The 2025 study of 4,323 YC companies found observable founder-background variables explained less than 4% of funding variation. Therefore the Atlas should model **capability and insight**, not prestige.
