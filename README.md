# Venture Atlas — Complete Research Edition

**Status:** Completed research edition v1.0  
**Date:** 22 August 2026

Venture Atlas is a standalone research project for reverse-engineering how elite startup investors identify venture-scale companies and converting those observations into testable founder, market and investment frameworks.

## Evidence standard

- **FACT:** directly supported by an official source or clearly documented dataset.
- **OBSERVATION:** repeated pattern in the evidence.
- **INFERENCE:** explanation derived from multiple observations.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** testable claim that could be disproved.

The Atlas does not claim to have discovered any investor's secret scoring algorithm.

## Structure

- Part I — Y Combinator research and selection model
- 2026 YC batch analysis and company profiles
- Investor comparison: Sequoia, a16z, HF0, Entrepreneurs First, NFX, General Catalyst
- Founder patterns and scoring framework
- Market, opportunity and white-space maps
- Founder playbook
- Final synthesis and references

## Research principle

The central model is that elite investors are **uncertainty-reduction machines**: each has a different mechanism for underwriting founder potential, market potential, execution risk, technology leverage or compounding advantage.
