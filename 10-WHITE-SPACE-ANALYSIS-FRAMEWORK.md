# Venture Atlas
# 10 - White Space Analysis Framework

Version 0.1 (Research Scaffold)

---

# Purpose

This framework identifies opportunities that are *not* yet heavily funded or served. The goal is to systematically search for promising markets before they become obvious.

---

# Definition

White Space is an opportunity where:

- Customer demand exists or is emerging.
- Existing solutions are inadequate.
- Technology enables a better approach.
- Competition remains limited.

---

# Research Questions

- Which customer problems remain unsolved?
- Which industries are underfunded?
- Which technologies are creating new possibilities?
- Which markets are overlooked by major investors?
- What assumptions do incumbents make that can be challenged?

---

# White Space Evaluation

## Customer Pain
- Frequency
- Severity
- Willingness to pay

## Market Readiness
- Technology maturity
- Infrastructure availability
- Regulatory environment
- Adoption barriers

## Competitive Density
- Number of funded startups
- Strength of incumbents
- Switching costs

## Founder Advantage
- Unique insight
- Proprietary data
- Distribution advantage
- Technical differentiation

---

# Opportunity Matrix

Every opportunity should be classified by:

- Industry
- Customer
- Problem
- Technology
- Estimated Market Size
- Competition Level
- AI Potential
- Time to MVP
- Capital Intensity

---

# White Space Scorecard

Rate each opportunity (1–10):

- Customer Pain
- Timing
- AI Leverage
- Competitive Gap
- Founder Advantage
- Venture Potential

Include a written justification for every score.

---

# Deliverables

For each sector, produce:

- High-confidence white-space opportunities
- Supporting evidence
- Risks and assumptions
- Why now?
- Candidate startup ideas
