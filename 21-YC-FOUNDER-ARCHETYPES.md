# 21 — YC Founder Archetypes
The 2025 study of 4,323 YC companies found observable founder-background variables explain less than 4% of funding variation. Larger founding teams were more consistently associated with more capital raised.

### Useful descriptive archetypes
1. Technical builder
2. Domain operator
3. Serial founder
4. Research/frontier founder

These are descriptive, not deterministic.

### Founder Edge Score™
Measure capability rather than prestige:
- Build ability: 0–5
- Domain insight: 0–5
- Execution history: 0–5
- Team complementarity: 0–5
- Learning velocity: 0–5

### Key conclusion
The evidence supports “YC contains many capable technical and experienced founders,” not “elite credentials cause YC success.”

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13755
