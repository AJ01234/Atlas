# 24 — What YC Appears Less Excited About
The earlier Atlas used “avoids” too aggressively. Better language is:

> areas with lower historical representation or unusually difficult public investment signals.

### Examples
- **Education:** difficult buyer/economic structure highlighted in public partner commentary.
- **Consumer IoT:** incumbent advantages can make small hardware products difficult.
- **Capital-intensive/slow-cycle businesses:** long development cycles can fit less naturally with YC's rapid iteration model.
- **Cannabis:** historical regulatory constraints show that “avoidance” can change with institutions and law.

### What this does not claim
It does not claim YC never funds these sectors.

### Falsifiability
If future cohorts materially increase funding in a supposedly underweighted category, reclassify it as a changing thesis.
