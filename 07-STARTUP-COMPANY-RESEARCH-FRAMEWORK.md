# Venture Atlas
# 07 - Startup Company Research Framework

Version 0.1 (Research Scaffold)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the standard template that every startup profile in the Venture Atlas will follow. A consistent structure makes companies comparable across investors, industries, and time.

---

# Company Snapshot

- Company Name
- Batch / Investor
- Founding Year
- Headquarters
- Founders
- Stage
- Industry
- Website

---

# Problem

- What problem is being solved?
- Who experiences this problem?
- How painful is it?
- Why has it remained unsolved?

---

# Solution

- Product overview
- Key features
- Technical approach
- Why it is different

---

# Customer

- Primary customer
- Secondary customer
- Buyer vs user
- Ideal customer profile

---

# Market

- TAM
- SAM
- SOM
- Market growth
- Long-term opportunity

---

# Competition

- Direct competitors
- Indirect competitors
- Incumbents
- Competitive positioning

---

# Business Model

- Revenue model
- Pricing
- Sales motion
- Go-to-market strategy

---

# Technology

- AI usage
- Proprietary technology
- Data advantage
- Infrastructure requirements

---

# Founder Analysis

- Founder-market fit
- Previous experience
- Technical depth
- Domain expertise

---

# Investment Thesis

Why would an investor back this company?

Evaluate:
- Market timing
- Founder quality
- Execution potential
- Defensibility
- Scale potential

---

# Risks

Document major risks such as:
- Competition
- Regulation
- Customer adoption
- Technical feasibility
- Capital intensity

---

# Investment Scorecard

Score each category from 1–10.

- Problem Severity
- Founder-Market Fit
- Market Size
- Technical Moat
- AI Leverage
- Distribution
- Defensibility
- Timing
- Venture Scale

Include a brief justification for each score.
