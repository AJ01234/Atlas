# 20 — The AI Shift
AI is now a cross-cutting layer across YC's portfolio.

**FACT:** YC's current directory contains hundreds of AI-tagged companies and many adjacent AI categories. **FACT:** 2026 RFS explicitly frames AI as moving into the physical world and across education, healthcare, defense, finance, infrastructure and work.

### Stronger thesis
The Atlas should not say “YC funds AI.” A more defensible formulation is:

> YC appears especially interested when a new technical capability changes the economics of a large, painful workflow.

```text
Messy workflow → human coordination → new technical capability
→ automation/intelligence → lower cost/higher throughput → venture scale
```

### AI quality test
- Does AI materially improve the product?
- Does it change unit economics?
- Does it unlock a previously impractical workflow?
- Is there proprietary data/feedback?
- Does usage strengthen defensibility?
