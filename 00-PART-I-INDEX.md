### Methodological corrections
- RFS absence is not proof of rejection.
- Founder pedigree is not treated as causal.
- AI is a cross-cutting technology layer, not a mutually exclusive sector.
- Unicorns are treated as a selected outcome sample, not a representative portfolio.
- “Avoidance” means low historical representation or difficult public signals, not a permanent blacklist.
- Historical Series-A conversion figures are not treated as current cohort constants.
