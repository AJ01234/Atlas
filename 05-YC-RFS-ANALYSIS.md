# Purpose

The Requests for Startups (RFS) represent areas where Y Combinator publicly signals high interest. This chapter analyzes those requests without assuming they are strict investment requirements.

---

# Research Questions

- What is the purpose of RFS?
- How often are RFS themes updated?
- Which requests later become funded companies?
- Which themes persist across years?
- Which requests disappear?
- What macro trends influence RFS?

---

# Analysis Framework

For every RFS topic we will document:

- Problem statement
- Why now?
- Target customer
- Market opportunity
- Existing competitors
- Technical challenges
- AI opportunity
- Potential business models

---

# Cross-Reference

Each RFS topic will later be linked to:

- YC companies
- Sequoia investments
- a16z investments
- Emerging startups

This will help determine whether investor interest extends beyond YC.
