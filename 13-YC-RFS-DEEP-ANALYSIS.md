# 13 — YC Requests for Startups
## Executive thesis
**FACT:** YC says Requests for Startups represent only a fraction of what it funds. **OBSERVATION:** 2026 RFS themes heavily feature AI, physical-world AI/robotics, infrastructure, defense, education, finance and healthcare. **INFERENCE:** RFS is a leading indicator of partner curiosity, not a checklist.

### Research rule
Do not infer “YC does not fund X” merely because X is absent from RFS.

### 2026 signal
Recurring themes include AI moving into physical systems, AI-native education, defense, compute/infrastructure, collaborative AI, compliance/identity, robotics and AI for aging/health.

### Falsifiability
Test whether repeated RFS themes predict subsequent portfolio composition, partner hiring or funded-company categories.

**Primary source:** https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs
