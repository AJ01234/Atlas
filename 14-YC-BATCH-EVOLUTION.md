# 14 — YC Batch Evolution
YC evolved from an eight-company experiment in 2005 into a high-throughput global accelerator.

| Batch | Applications | Funded | Acceptance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| W22 | 17,000 | 414 | 2.44% |
| S22 | 19,000 | 240 | 1.26% |
| W23 | 20,000 | 282 | 1.41% |
| S23 | 24,000 | 229 | 0.95% |
| W24 | 27,000 | 260 | 0.96% |

**OBSERVATION:** Application growth has outpaced funded-company growth, pushing acceptance toward ~1% in recent published batches.

**INFERENCE:** YC's advantage increasingly depends on institutional machinery: standardized selection, partner groups, software, founder networks and repeatable support.

### Falsifiability
Compare founder outcomes before and after major scaling changes to test whether higher throughput degraded support or outcomes.

**Sources:** https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/ and https://www.ycombinator.com/apply/
