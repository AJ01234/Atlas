# 23 — YC Global Geography
YC is no longer meaningfully described as a Silicon Valley-only accelerator.

Historical batch reports show substantial international participation, with India, the UK and Nigeria recurring among non-U.S. ecosystems.

**OBSERVATION:** International founders form a substantial share of YC cohorts.

**INFERENCE:** YC's selection and network have become globally portable.

### Global Reach Index™
A future metric should compare YC representation with:
- startup formation
- venture funding
- technical talent
- population/GDP

Do not infer investment thesis from country counts alone.

**Sources:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies and https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/
