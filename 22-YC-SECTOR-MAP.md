### Critical taxonomy rule
AI is a technology layer, not a mutually exclusive sector. A company can be B2B + fintech + AI + automation.

### White Space Index™
Potential white space requires:
- high customer pain
- large market
- low YC/VC activity
- improving technology
- survivable barriers

Low YC representation alone is not proof of opportunity.
