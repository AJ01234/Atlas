# 16 — YC Demo Day & Fundraising
Demo Day is the transition from YC's internal founder-development environment to a broad investor market.

```text
YC acceptance → Build/iterate → Demo Day → Seed fundraising → Growth evidence → Series A / acquisition / shutdown
```

**FACT:** YC has promoted fast fundraising mechanisms such as the handshake protocol.

**INFERENCE:** Demo Day is an attention-compression mechanism, not a guarantee of capital.

The earlier “~30% Series A” statistic should be treated as historical partner commentary, not as a universal 2026 conversion rate.

### Part II test
For each cohort, track funding within 3/6/12 months, Series A within 24 months, acquisition/IPO and inactive status.
