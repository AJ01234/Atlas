# 17 — YC Partner & Organizational Structure
YC has evolved from a founder-led experiment into an institution with executive leadership, partners and specialized operational teams.

**FACT:** The public YC organization includes CEO leadership, General/Managing/Group Partners and operational functions.

### Why this matters
A scaled accelerator needs:
- standardized application processing
- partner-led selection
- batch organization
- founder support infrastructure
- investor operations
- software systems
- alumni/network effects

**INFERENCE:** YC is partly an operating system for company formation, not merely a seed fund.

### Falsifiability
Compare founder outcomes before and after major organizational changes.

**Source:** https://www.ycombinator.com/
